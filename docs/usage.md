# Usage

Open Teams in the browser, then click the extension's toolbar icon to open the side panel.

## Upload

1. Click **Add Emoji Files** and pick one or more images. Each gets a preview and a `:name:` taken from its file name.
2. Change any names you want. Names can't contain spaces or colons, and must be unique.
3. Click **Upload to Teams**. Up to 10 files upload at once, each with its own progress bar.

If a file fails, its row shows why, for example when the name is already taken. Rename it and click **Upload to Teams** again; only the files that haven't uploaded are sent.

The list is kept if you close the panel, and an upload carries on in the background.

## Browse and delete

1. Open **Browse & delete**. Use the search box, or tick **Only mine** to see just the emojis you added.
2. Click the trash icon on an emoji, or tick several and click **Delete selected**.
3. Check the confirmation dialog, which lists everything that will be deleted, and confirm. Deleting 10 or more also asks you to type `delete`.

Teams allows each user 10 deletions per minute. Bigger deletes pause with a countdown and carry on by themselves. Anything that fails is marked on its tile and stays selected, so you can retry.

## Seeing changes in Teams

Teams caches its emoji list, so new or deleted emojis may not show up straight away.

- **Refresh Teams** at the bottom of the panel clears Teams' caches and reloads it. You stay signed in.
- **Full reset** clears all of Teams' site data, which signs you out. Use it if a refresh isn't enough.

Teams can also take a while to sync changes on its side.
